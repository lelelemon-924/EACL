7 Conclusion 600
Root-level refusal does not determine whether a 601
model recovers once generation has entered an un- 602
safe state. Holding that state fixed exposes a com- 603
mon preference reversal that clean-prompt training 604
only weakly addresses, and the lower-tail recovery 605
margin predicts held-out attack success far better 606
than root-level diagnostics. CURE-DPO turns that 607
diagnosis into a training signal by changing what 608
the model compares, not how it is scored. 609
