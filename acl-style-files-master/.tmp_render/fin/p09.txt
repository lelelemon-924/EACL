610 Limitations
611 Our threat model concerns recovery from unsafe
612 generation states, not prevention of every jailbreak.
613 LTRM and the recovery margins are predictive
614 statistics over sampled states, not certificates: they
615 bound nothing over the full reachable-state space,
616 and an adversary can construct states outside our
617 sampling distribution. The adaptive attack of Sec￾618 tion 5.2 narrows this gap but does not close it: it
619 adapts to the defended checkpoint rather than to the
620 defense, so an attacker who used a recovery margin
621 to search directly for weakly recovered states is a
622 stronger threat model that we leave to future work.
623 The harmful and recovery branches are con￾624 structed alternatives. Although we filter for intent
625 match, audit a sample of pairs, and evaluate on
626 an unseen harmful-response source, the harmful
627 source and the recovery policy jointly shape what
628 “recovery” means, and a different source could shift
629 both the mined hard states and the learned behavior.
630 Our scope is two English, text-only instruction
631 models (Qwen3.5-9B and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct),
632 one full-parameter recipe, and three seeds. Three
633 seeds support only weak seed-level inference,
634 which is why we report seed and prompt-level un￾635 certainty separately instead of pooling them into
636 one interval. We do not evaluate other languages,
637 modalities, larger models, or reasoning-style gener￾638 ation.
639 Depth extrapolation is tested to 512 tokens; be￾640 havior at the multi-thousand token states typical
641 of long agentic traces is untested. The naturally
642 occurring suite depends on an evaluator to locate
643 the first unsafe span, so states whose harmfulness is
644 encoded or metaphorical are under-represented in
645 it—the same blind spot that appears in our failure
646 analysis.
647 Our over-refusal measurement covers behavior
648 decided at or near the root of a short response:
649 ORR on XSTest and OR-Bench-Hard, plus IFE￾650 val and MMLU-Pro. One failure mode specific
651 to same-state training therefore falls outside it—
652 a model taught to abandon a trajectory it has be￾653 gun could in principle abandon a benign long-form
654 generation mid-way, where the response starts cor￾655 rectly and truncation happens later. Measuring mid￾656 generation behavior on benign long-form prompts
657 is left to future work.
658 In our experiments, stronger safety carries a mea￾659 surable helpfulness cost. CURE-DPO increases
660 over-refusal on benign professional prompts con￾taining high-risk terminology (Table 16 shows one 661
such case), and tightening safety further would 662
raise that cost. We report over-refusal and utility 663
next to every safety number so the trade-off stays 664
explicit. 665
