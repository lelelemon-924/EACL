503 6.2 Main Comparison
504 Table 1 reports the primary model. Clean DPO
505 lowers clean-prompt attack success to 2.2% yet
506 leaves middle-insertion attack success at 52.4%:
507 a stronger root refusal does not solve recovery.
508 Ranking the recovery branch against its compet￾509 ing harmful future matters more than maximizing
510 the recovery target alone (Uniform Same-State vs.
511 Recovery SFT, −11.3 pp), and lower-tail aggre￾512 gation adds more (H2: CURE-DPO vs. Uniform,
513 −5.9 pp, [−7.9, −3.9]).
514 Every external row is retrained under one recipe;
515 Appendix I documents method-specific adaptations.
516 DOOR and W-DOOR (Zhao et al., 2025) reinforce
517 a safe continuation and unlearn harmful knowledge
518 but do not directly optimize a pairwise preference
519 between continuations from the identical state. W￾520 DOOR is the strongest reproduced external com￾521 parator; the internal factorial more directly isolates
522 pairwise ranking. PRESTO (Vega and Singh, 2025)
523 trains against a rank-based rather than a probability￾524 based safety target and closes less of the gap.
525 6.3 What Causes the Gain
526 Table 2 crosses pair construction with objective.
527 Two effects separate cleanly. Moving the condi￾528 tioning context from the root prompt to the reached
529 state changes middle ASR by 26.5 pp under DPO,
530 22.5 under SimPO, and 19.9 under SFT: root￾531 conditioned supervision, whatever objective car￾532 ries it, leaves most of the mid-generation failure
533 untouched.
534 The second contrast concerns pairwise ranking
535 at a fixed conditioning context. Changing only the
objective moves middle ASR by 4.7 pp at the root 536
and 11.3 pp at the reached state. Recovery SFT 537
raises the likelihood of a designated safe contin- 538
uation without ranking it against the competing 539
harmful future and has 11.3 pp higher ASR than 540
same-state DPO. SimPO’s per-branch length nor- 541
malization, which breaks the prefix cancellation 542
of Appendix A, likewise costs 1.5 pp at the root 543
but 5.5 pp at a reached state. In this factorial, ob- 544
jective choice has a larger effect under reached- 545
state conditioning; lower-tail state selection adds 546
5.9 pp (Section 6.2). The prefix-unmasked control 547
matches its masked twin to within 0.1 pp, consis- 548
tent with masking being an implementation choice 549
rather than the source of the gain. 550
