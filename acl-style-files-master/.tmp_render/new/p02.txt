2 Related Work 133
Safety alignment and evaluation. Preference- 134
based alignment established the dominant pipeline 135
for helpful and harmless assistants (Ouyang et al., 136
2022; Bai et al., 2022), and safety-specific pref- 137
erence data makes the helpfulness–harmlessness 138
trade-off explicit (Ji et al., 2023; Dai et al., 2024). 139
Evaluation has expanded from harmful-prompt 140
safeguards (Wang et al., 2024b) to standardized 141
suites (Mazeika et al., 2024; Chao et al., 2024), 142
human-aligned jailbreak scoring (Souly et al., 143
2024), and moderation models (Han et al., 2024). 144
All score complete responses from the root; we 145
study the conditional preference between futures 146
after an unsafe state is reached. 147
