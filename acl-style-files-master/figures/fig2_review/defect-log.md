## Cycle 1 — full visual reset

### Inventory
- **Typography:** 32 inline Comic Sans declarations conflicted with the ACL serif
  body; circled Unicode stage numerals looked informal; body/sub-label hierarchy
  was weak.
- **Arrow hygiene:** stage arrows were visually heavy; branch arrows competed with
  the main pipeline; line-weight semantics were inconsistent.
- **Box integrity:** four large outline-only cards created a dashboard/wireframe
  appearance; the loss card was visually hollow; state and anchor boxes relied

  lacked a clear shared top rule; lower annotations competed with the frozen band.

  the handwritten font overrode the specified Times family.
- **Layout:** stage 2 was wider but not perceptually dominant; stages 3–4 looked as
  novel as stages 1–2.
- **Icons/glyphs:** circled numerals and the mixed-font check/cross treatment felt
  inconsistent with the rest of the paper.
- **Style coherence:** overall rendering resembled a draw.io sketch rather than a
  camera-ready vector figure.

- Replaced circled Unicode numerals with restrained numbered badges.
- Added the guide's semantic fills to state, safe, harmful, selected-loss, anchor,

## Cycle 5 — legend size and export geometry

- **Legend was the smallest thing in the figure.** Line samples were 34–40 px
  long (about 8 pt once printed) and labels were 17 px. Samples are now 66 px at
  5.0 pt / 4.4 pt stroke and labels are 24 px, taking the labels from 3.97 pt to
  5.61 pt on the page. Because the legend sits below the panels, enlarging it
  costs no panel space and does not change any other element's printed size.
- **Legend grouping.** All four elements had ~21 px gaps, so `optimized` read as
  ambiguously paired with the dashed sample. Within-pair gaps are now ~19 px and
  the between-pair gap ~45 px, so the key reads as two entries, not four items.
- **Export page geometry was wrong twice over.** The exporter hardcoded a
  6.90 in × 1.95 in page, but (a) `\textwidth` is 455.24 pt = 6.323 in, so LaTeX
  was silently rescaling every figure by 0.9159, and (b) the drawing's own
  aspect ratio made it 1.68 in tall inside a 1.95 in page, letterboxing it with
  0.271 in of dead space that was then scaled along with the artwork. The
  exporter now sizes the page to `\textwidth` and derives the height from the
  SVG's intrinsic aspect ratio. The PDF bounding box now fills the page exactly,
  LaTeX applies no rescaling, and the figure gives back 0.27 in of page height.

### Known open issue — not a defect introduced here

Even after cycle 5 the figure's text is well below what the figure guide
specifies. On the page 1 canvas px = 0.2337 pt, so panel titles are 5.84 pt
against a specified 8 pt, body labels 4.21 pt against 7 pt, and sub-labels
3.74 pt against 6 pt. This predates cycle 4 and cannot be fixed by changing
font sizes alone: enlarging text enlarges the boxes and therefore the canvas,
leaving the ratio unchanged. Reaching ~6 pt for body text needs roughly 1.4×
the linear scale, which means the printed figure has to grow from 1.54 in to
about 2.2 in tall — i.e. panels that are narrower and taller rather than four
wide flat ones — or less content per panel.

### Accepted deviation from the figure guide

## Cycle 9 — panel 1: the funnel became a margin chart

The 11 → 6 → 3 → 2 → 1 funnel drew the *procedure* (successive halving's
elimination rounds) while the caption under its winner, `lowest base margin`,
named the *criterion*. Both were true, since the tournament's objective is that
minimum, but the reader had to bridge them, and nothing in the panel said that the
vertical direction meant rounds. Readers asked what the funnel meant, which is the
symptom that matters.

Replaced with eleven bars on a common baseline at y 356, spanning x 101..303 on a
19 px pitch, heights `46 38 50 30 42 14 34 24 44 28 36`. Now the graphic and the
caption describe one thing: the bars *are* base margins and the red bar *is* the
lowest one, so `lowest base margin` needs no bridging and the panel needs no
explanatory note. The criterion is also the scientifically load-bearing half — it
connects to Eq. (7) and to \ltrm{} — whereas halving is the budget story, which
Appendix A already states with the 2/2/4 pulls and the 66-evaluation total.

Two deliberate choices:

- **The minimum sits at index 5, the middle bar**, whose centre is x 202.0, the
  panel axis. That makes `lowest base margin` simultaneously attached to the red
  bar and centred on the panel, so no alignment is traded for attachment. It also
  avoids the misreading that the deepest position always wins: had the shortest
  bar been at the right end, the panel would imply one can simply take the deepest
  state and skip the search, which removes stage 1's reason to exist.
- **The winner is encoded twice**, solid `#C0392B` fill against pale outlined
  bars, and shortest of the eleven. Verified on a grayscale render: the red bar is
  the only dark solid one and is unmistakably the shortest, so neither cue alone

The connector still rises from the winner, runs right at y 290 with 16 px of
clearance above the tallest bar, and turns up at x 322, the exact centre of the
`hard` label. Panel 1 drops from 25 cells to 15. `MediaBox` unchanged, 23 pages.

`11 positions` is kept: it is the only thing reconciling six ticks on the axis
with eleven bars in the chart, which was the original defect this panel had.

## Cycle 10 — full text-versus-figure audit

Every text cell was extracted and checked against the manuscript. Two genuine
discrepancies and three clarity defects were found and fixed.

**Discrepancy 1 — a source was missing.** `u_t: held-in harmful response` named
only one of two sources. Both §4.1 and Appendix K say harmful branches *combine*
PKU-SafeRLHF/BeaverTails responses *with* generations from a model tuned on the
held-in harmful subset. The figure silently dropped the public half. Now
`from branch pool`, after §4.5's own collective noun `harmful-branch pool`.

**Discrepancy 2 — the score row abused notation.** `A_θ = π_θ − π_ref` reads as
subtracting two policies, which is meaningless; Eq. (6) subtracts the two
*summed log-likelihoods*. Cycle 8c had removed the figure's only `Σ log`, leaving
this row with no way to be read correctly. The chips now carry
`Σ log π_θ` and `Σ log π_ref`, i.e. Eq. (6) itself. This also restores the
paper's stated choice of the summed rather than length-normalized score — and in a
position where a formula is grammatically right, unlike the caption slot cycle 8c
correctly rejected. Chips widened 56 → 98 px, `A_θ =` narrowed 56 → 50 px and the
minus and second chip re-spaced, leaving 26 px (5.96 pt) before
`→ canonical DPO`; the arrow is kept.

**Clarity 1 — `detached` under-specified the criterion.** A reader who knows
stage 1 mined `t_hard` expects it in the top-2 and is surprised it is not, because
`detached` says only "no gradient", not "of the current model, recomputed on every
sample". Now `ranked by current detached loss`.

**Clarity 2 — the note-box pattern had gone inconsistent.** Cycles 8–9 left
panels 1 and 3 box-free while panel 2 still carried a detached two-line yellow
note. Both source lines moved *inside* their branch boxes as second lines at
18 px gray, matching how `pair_state` already sets `s_t` over `identical state`.
Removing the note left panel 2 bottom-heavy, so its four vertices were re-centred:
branch centres 197.0 and 305.0, and both `pair_state` and `pair_anchor` centred at
251.0, exactly their midpoint. White is 92 px above and below. The three edges are
cell-attached and followed automatically.

**Clarity 3 — two names for one checkpoint.** The figure said `frozen-base` in
panel 2 but `π_ref` in panels 3 and 4. Renaming everything to `π_ref` was
rejected: the appendix's mining score is explicitly "the reference-free,
continuous-length-normalized score", so calling panel 1's quantity a `π_ref`
margin would imply it is reference-adjusted — a worse error than the one being
fixed. The paper's two symbols encode two *roles* of one checkpoint. Fixed instead
by binding them once in the caption, which now reads "The frozen base model---the
same checkpoint that later serves as \(\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}\)---writes every
recovery branch...". Panel 2's label became `from π_ref`, so `base` now survives
in the figure only in panel 1, where it is the correct role name.

The caption's gradient sentence also became "dashed outlines mark the *model
components* that receive no gradient", because only two elements are dashed while
much else in the figure is frozen-model-derived data rather than a component.
