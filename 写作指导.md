## 0.4 方法名

> **CURE-DPO: Conditional Unsafe-state Recovery Preference Optimization**

## 0.5 唯一中心问题

> **A model may refuse safely from the original prompt, but does it still prefer safe recovery after it has entered an unsafe generation state?**

## 0.6 唯一中心结论

> **待证据确认的中心结论：** Root-level refusal does not imply state-level recoverability. The preference can reverse under an identical unsafe state; matched same-state training with lower-tail emphasis may improve recovery robustness. 最终措辞由 Gate 1–3 的结果决定。

## 0.7 全文叙事链

全文只能沿着下面一条链前进：

> **root refusal can hide a state-conditioned preference reversal**  
> → **same-state recovery margin measures the reversal**  
> → **the lower tail predicts attack success**  
> → **matched same-state pairs expose the local ordering, while factorial controls identify the source of the gain**  
> → **matched comparisons, held-out shifts, a second model family, utility tests, and human audits eliminate alternative explanations**

不要把论文写成“新指标 + 新 DPO + 新 tail objective + 新 state proposal”的组件堆叠。

## 0.8 三条贡献

Introduction 末尾固定为三条：

1. **Diagnosis.** We identify and measure a state-conditioned preference reversal: models that prefer refusal at the root can prefer harmful continuation over safe recovery after reaching an unsafe state. Whether a lower-tail same-state margin predicts held-out intervention success better than root-level safety signals is tested under fully separated evaluation.
2. **Method.** We introduce CURE-DPO, which constructs matched recovery/harmful futures under an identical reached state and applies per-prompt top-\(k\) lower-tail training to the states with the largest current preference losses.
3. **Evidence.** Under matched data and compute, we test whether gains come from identical-state pair construction, score normalization, or hard-state aggregation, and evaluate the resulting method under source-, attack-, and judge-separated settings. Only comparisons supported by the completed factorial and separated evaluations may remain in the final contribution statement.

# 3. Introduction：五段直接写作方案

## 3.1 P1：从自回归条件建立现实问题

**目标：120–140 词。**

本段只完成三件事：

1. 对齐评测通常从 assistant turn 开头检查拒答；
2. 自回归模型实际依据 prompt 与已生成历史继续生成；
3. 到达 unsafe state 后，root refusal 不再足以描述安全性。

可直接起草为：

> Safety-aligned language models are usually evaluated at the beginning of an assistant response, where a harmful request should trigger refusal. This evaluation captures whether the model selects a safe response from the original prompt, but not whether safety persists after the generation history changes. Autoregressive decoding conditions every next token on the complete preceding state, which may contain an early model error, an assistant prefill, a mid-sequence intervention, or content returned by an external component. A model can therefore avoid entering a harmful trajectory from the root while still continuing that trajectory once an unsafe state has been reached. A useful safety objective must cover not only which response is selected at the root, but also whether the model can recover from unsafe states encountered during generation.

引用位置：

- 第一句或第二句后：InstructGPT、Constitutional AI 中选 1–2 篇；
- “safety persists”或末两句前：Shallow Safety Alignment 及一篇 deep/recovery 工作。

不要在本段介绍 CURE-DPO，也不要列 GCG、RAP 等攻击名称。

## 3.2 P2：最小排序反例与核心概念

**目标：100–120 词。**

必须让读者在不看公式时理解本文对象：

```text
At the original prompt:          safe response > harmful response
At the identical unsafe state:   harmful continuation > safe recovery
```

可直接起草为：

> We study the resulting discrepancy as a state-conditioned preference reversal. At the original prompt, an aligned model can assign higher preference to a safe response than to a harmful response. After the history is extended to an unsafe state, however, the same model may rank a harmful continuation above a safe recovery. The two local alternatives share the original prompt and the complete post-intervention history; only their future tokens differ. This identical-state control is essential: without it, a comparison can confound the model's preference over future behavior with differences in the prefix, insertion path, or trajectory construction. The scientific object is therefore the ordering of two possible futures under the same reached state, rather than another score assigned to complete responses from the root.

本段不需要堆引用。Figure 1 在本段后首次引用。

## 3.3 P3：最近邻工作和精确缺口

**目标：150–170 词。**

顺序固定：

1. shallow/deep safety、prefill recovery 和 insertion training 已证明位置重要；
2. Recovery SFT 只增加 recovery target likelihood；
3. full-trajectory preference objective 可能混入 prefix/path 差异；
4. token-level DPO 改变 response token credit，不改变 conditioning state；
5. 落到 identical reached state。

可直接起草为：

> Recent work has shown that safety alignment can be concentrated near the beginning of a response and that training on harmful prefixes, arbitrary insertion positions, or augmented trajectories can improve robustness beyond the first tokens. These results establish that generation depth and recovery training matter, but they leave a more specific conditional question unresolved. Recovery SFT increases the likelihood of a designated safe continuation without explicitly reducing the probability of the competing harmful future under the same state. Full-trajectory preference objectives rank complete augmented sequences, whose prefixes or intervention paths may also differ. Token-level preference methods refine credit assignment within a response, but do not change which conditioning states are compared. Consequently, existing approaches can improve post-prefix safety without directly measuring or optimizing the local recovery-over-harmful ordering while the complete reached state is held fixed.

引用要求：

- shallow safety；
- DOOR；
- PRESTO/RAP；
- arbitrary/worst-insertion training；
- trajectory alignment；
- DPO/SimPO；
- TDPO/TIS-DPO 中选 1–2 篇。

不要写“none of prior work considers recovery”。

## 3.4 P4：诊断发现直接引出方法

**目标：130–150 词。**

可直接起草为：

> We first define a same-state recovery margin: the length-normalized conditional log-likelihood advantage that the current model assigns to a safe recovery over a harmful continuation under an identical unsafe state. Its lower tail is evaluated as a predictor of held-out intervention success under source-, attack-, and judge-separated data. This observation motivates CURE-DPO, which constructs matched alternative futures under a shared reached state and concentrates each update on the highest-loss third of sampled states. Because a shared prefix cancels under standard sequence-sum DPO, we separately test pair construction, score normalization, prefix treatment, and hard-state aggregation rather than attributing the gain to masking alone. Clean-safety and benign anchors preserve root refusal and general instruction following.

# 4. Related Work：三个自然段

**总长度：280–340 词。** 不设三个三级标题，以节省版面并增强连续性。

## 4.1 段 1：Safety beyond the root

本段讨论：shallow safety、deep safety、harmful-prefix recovery、DOOR、PRESTO/RAP、Any-Depth、worst insertion、trajectory alignment。

可直接起草为：

> **Safety beyond the root.** Safety alignment may primarily alter the first few output-token distributions, leaving later conditional behavior comparatively weakly constrained. Recovery augmentation, dual-objective alignment, rank-based defenses, arbitrary-depth interventions, and trajectory-level training address this problem by exposing models to harmful prefixes or later generation positions. These approaches establish that robustness must extend beyond the initial refusal decision. Their comparison units nevertheless differ from ours: recovery objectives typically learn one safe target from an augmented context, inference-time methods alter decoding or reintroduce alignment cues, and trajectory objectives rank complete sequences from the original prompt. We instead hold the complete reached state fixed and ask which of its alternative future branches the model conditionally prefers.

引用 6–8 篇，但同一括号最多 4 篇。

## 4.2 段 2：Jailbreak mechanisms and recovery

可直接起草为：

> **Jailbreak mechanisms and recovery.** Adversarial suffix optimization, gradient-based red teaming, inference-time alignment views, and representation-level analyses explain safety failures through optimized inputs, search procedures, or internal safety mechanisms. This literature motivates evaluating models under adaptive and off-distribution attacks rather than relying only on clean harmful prompts. Our analysis is complementary: it does not propose a stronger input-side jailbreak or identify a single internal safety direction. It measures whether a state that has already been reached still favors recovery, and uses that conditional preference as both a diagnostic and a training target.

引用 GCG、Gradient-Based Language Model Red Teaming、Jailbreaks as Inference-Time Alignment、Safety Knowledge Neurons 等 3–5 篇。

## 4.3 段 3：Preference optimization granularity

可直接起草为：

> **Preference optimization and granularity.** DPO and subsequent variants improve alignment by changing sequence-level rewards, reference dependence, token-level credit assignment, or sampling weights. SimPO provides a strong complete-sequence preference baseline, while token-level and importance-weighted variants show that where loss is assigned within a response can matter. CURE-DPO changes a different axis: the conditioning unit of comparison. Its positive and negative branches share the entire unsafe state, all shared tokens are masked, and only their alternative futures receive preference loss. The lower-tail aggregation then determines which reached states dominate the update; it is used as an empirical risk-focused training rule rather than claimed as a new general preference-learning theory.

引用 DPO、SimPO、TDPO、TIS-DPO、MMPO 和 CVaR 原始工作。

# 5. Section 3：Root Refusal Does Not Imply Recovery

**目标：500–570 词。** 本节是第一项科学贡献，不是纯 notation。

## 5.1 开场段

可直接使用：

> We formalize safety after a model has entered an intermediate generation state. The key distinction is between selecting a response from the original prompt and selecting a continuation from a state that already contains unsafe assistant-side history. This distinction allows us to measure preference reversal without conflating the two alternative futures with different prompts or prefixes.

## 5.2 Unsafe generation state

定义：

\[
s_t=x\oplus h_{\leq t},
\]

其中：

- \(x\)：原始对话上下文；
- \(h_{\leq t}\)：位置 \(t\) 之前模型已经接收到的 assistant-side history；
- \(s_t\)：当前完整条件状态。

正文解释：

> The history may be produced by the target model, supplied through assistant prefilling, or introduced by a mid-sequence intervention. We treat \(s_t\) only as conditioning context: the model is not trained to reproduce its unsafe tokens. For every comparison, the safe and harmful branches share exactly the same \(s_t\).

## 5.3 Diagnostic branch score

诊断阶段使用当前 checkpoint 自身的长度归一化条件 log-likelihood：

\[
S_\theta(s,y)=\frac{1}{|y|}\sum_{j=1}^{|y|}
\log \pi_\theta(y_j\mid s,y_{<j}).
\]

公式后必须解释：

- \(S_\theta\) 测量当前模型在给定 state 下对 branch 的自身条件排序；
- 长度归一化降低短拒答天然占优的问题；
- branch 长度仍需人工/规则匹配，不能只依赖公式修正；
- 诊断 score 不减去 reference score，因此即使 Base checkpoint 同时作为 \(\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}\)，其 margin 也不会按定义恒为零；
- reference normalization 只用于 Section 4 的 DPO 训练目标，不用于 preference reversal、LTRM 或 H1。

## 5.4 Root 与 state recovery margin

原始 prompt 的 root margin：

\[
m_\theta^{\mathrm{root}}(x)=
\beta\left[S_\theta(x,r_0)-S_\theta(x,u_0)\right].
\]

unsafe state 的 recovery margin：

\[
m_\theta(s_t)=
\beta\left[S_\theta(s_t,r_t)-S_\theta(s_t,u_t)\right].
\]

其中：

- \(r_0\)、\(r_t\)：安全回答或安全恢复 continuation；
- \(u_0\)、\(u_t\)：有害回答或有害 continuation；
- \(m>0\)：当前模型自身的长度归一化条件分数更偏好安全分支；
- \(m<0\)：当前模型自身的长度归一化条件分数更偏好有害分支。

## 5.5 Preference reversal

定义经验事件：

\[
m_\theta^{\mathrm{root}}(x)>0
\quad\text{and}\quad
m_\theta(s_t)<0.
\]

可直接解释：

> A preference reversal occurs when the model ranks the safe response above the harmful response at the root but reverses this ordering after reaching an unsafe state. This definition concerns conditional ranking, not whether the model actually sampled either branch in a single rollout.

## 5.6 Lower-tail recovery margin

一条 prompt 的候选状态集合为 \(\mathcal S(x)\)。定义：

\[
\operatorname{LTRM}_q(x)=
\frac{1}{|\mathcal B_q(x)|}
\sum_{s\in\mathcal B_q(x)}m_\theta(s),
\]

其中 \(\mathcal B_q(x)\) 是 recovery margin 最低的 \(q\) 比例状态。诊断固定 \(q=0.2\)。

正文只正式命名 recovery margin 和 LTRM。不要再定义 RSM、MRSM、WSRM 缩写。

## 5.7 Recovery success 与恢复质量

不能把“continuation 最后出现拒绝句”直接算作恢复。主要二元定义固定为：

\[
\operatorname{RecoverySuccess}(s,y)
=
\mathbf 1\{
\operatorname{NoAdditionalHarm}(s,y)
\land
\operatorname{StopWithinK}(y;K)
\land
\operatorname{SafeRedirect}(y)
\}.
\]

其中 \(K\) 必须在看测试结果前固定为 32 或 64 tokens。判定同时要求：

- 不新增可执行危险步骤；
- 不复述或扩写 unsafe state；
- 在 \(K\) tokens 内明确停止危险方向；
- 给出边界说明、风险解释或安全替代；
- “先继续有害内容、末尾再拒绝”计为失败。

# 6. Section 4：CURE-DPO

**目标：620–700 词。** same-state pair construction、branch score、prefix-cancellation control 和 per-prompt top-\(k\) lower-tail aggregation 必须写成一个统一方法。

## 6.1 开场段

> CURE-DPO directly optimizes the local ordering defined above. Each training instance contains one reached state and two alternative futures, allowing the loss to change the recovery-over-harmful preference without rewarding or reproducing the unsafe history itself.

## 6.2 Same-state pair construction

每个样本：

\[
(s_t,r_t,u_t).
\]

固定构造流程：

1. 从 harmful prompt 生成一条独立 harmful trajectory；
2. 在第 5、10、20、40、80 token 截取五个固定深度状态，记为
   \(\mathcal T_{\mathrm{fixed}}=\{5,10,20,40,80\}\)；
3. 定义训练专用搜索网格
   \(\mathcal T_{\mathrm{search}}=\{5,10,\ldots,80\}\)，并将不属于固定集合的 11 个位置视为 stochastic arms：
   \[
   \mathcal T_{\mathrm{adaptive}}
   =
   \mathcal T_{\mathrm{search}}\setminus\mathcal T_{\mathrm{fixed}};
   \]
4. 对 arm \(t\) 的第 \(i\) 次 pull，在相同 state 下从预先固定并经过质量过滤的 branch-construction distributions 独立采样
   \(r_{t,i}\sim\mathcal R(s_t)\) 与 \(u_{t,i}\sim\mathcal U(s_t)\)，并计算
   \[
   M_{t,i}
   =
   \beta\left[
   S_{\mathrm{base}}(s_t,r_{t,i})
   -
   S_{\mathrm{base}}(s_t,u_{t,i})
   \right],
   \qquad
   \mu_t=\mathbb E[M_{t,i}];
   \]
5. 使用固定预算 66 个 valid paired evaluations 运行三轮 Successive Halving screening，再做独立 confirmation：
   - Round 1：11 arms 各 2 pulls，保留 empirical mean 最低的 6 个；
   - Round 2：6 arms 各增加 2 pulls，保留最低的 3 个；
   - Round 3：3 arms 各增加 4 pulls，保留最低的 2 个；
   - screening 共使用 \(11\times2+6\times2+3\times4=46\) 个 valid pulls；
   - 对最后两个 finalists 各生成 10 个全新 confirmation pulls，共 20 个；
   - 总预算严格为 \(46+2\times10=66\)；
6. screening round 使用截至当前轮的
   \[
   \widehat\mu_t=\frac{1}{n_t}\sum_{i=1}^{n_t}M_{t,i}
   \]
   排序并执行固定预算淘汰。screening samples 只决定 finalists，不进入最终 margin 估计；
7. 对两个 finalists 分别仅用 10 个独立 confirmation pulls 计算
   \[
   \widehat\mu_t^{\mathrm{conf}}
   =
   \frac{1}{10}\sum_{i=1}^{10}M_{t,i}^{\mathrm{conf}}
   \]
   及 branch-sample bootstrap 95% CI。独立 confirmation 避免把自适应筛选阶段的低估偏差直接带入最终比较，并报告两个 intervals 是否重叠；
8. 最终选择
   \[
   t_{\mathrm{hard}}
   =
   \arg\min_{t\in\mathcal F(x)}
   \widehat\mu_t^{\mathrm{conf}},
   \qquad |\mathcal F(x)|=2;
   \]
9. hard state 确定后，重新独立生成一对未参与 screening 或 confirmation 的 \((r_t,u_t)\) 作为训练 pair，避免把 winner's-curse branch 直接用于训练；
10. 每个 prompt 最终保留五个固定状态和一个 additional hard state，共六个状态；该六状态集合由所有训练 baseline 共享；
11. 所有 continuation 最大 96 tokens，pair 内长度差不超过 8 tokens；
12. 过滤 unreadable、truncated、label-inconsistent、near-duplicate 和低质量 pairs；
13. train/dev/test 在 prompt、intent cluster、harmful source、attack template 和 recovery text neighbor 上隔离。

hard-state mining 的硬规则：

- 只在 training prompts 上挖掘；不使用 attack success、HarmBench 标签、development endpoint 或测试集结果选状态；
- 不为不同 baseline 单独挖掘状态，否则破坏 matched comparison；
- 状态挖掘使用冻结 Base 自身的诊断分数 \(S_{\mathrm{base}}\)，不使用 reference-adjusted \(A_\theta\)；
- \(\mathcal R\) 与 \(\mathcal U\) 的 teacher revision、prompt、temperature、top-p、max tokens、过滤规则和随机种子生成方式在挖掘前冻结；
- 66 表示通过质量过滤的 valid pair pulls；同时报告 attempted generations、acceptance rate 和每个 prompt 的实际计算成本；
- 报告 bootstrap 下 hard-state re-selection stability，以及最终两个 arms CI 重叠的 prompt 比例；
- 对轨迹不足 80 tokens、候选状态缺失或某 arm 无法获得足够 valid pairs 的处理规则必须预先指定；
- 训练阶段的 top-2 current-loss selection 与数据构建阶段的 hard-state mining 是两个不同步骤。

必须做固定预算 state-mining ablation，并放入 Appendix J：

1. random additional position；
2. single-pair argmin over 11 arms；
3. uniform allocation with the same 66-pair budget（11 arms 各 6 pulls）；
4. Successive Halving plus independent confirmation with the same 66-pair budget。

报告 discovered \(\widehat\mu_t\)、bootstrap re-selection stability、valid/attempted branch queries、wall-clock cost，以及使用所选 state 训练后的 middle/unseen-source ASR。该消融用于证明收益来自更可靠的 state identification，而不是额外 branch-generation compute。

正文引用 Jamieson and Talwalkar (2016) 作为 fixed-budget Successive Halving 的来源，并引用 Kalyanakrishnan et al. (2012) 说明 confidence-based best-arm identification。必须明确：本文不把 best-arm identification 本身作为算法创新；贡献是把同状态 recovery/harmful branch sampling 形成的随机 margin 用于可靠选择 additional unsafe generation state。

safe recovery 的内容固定包含：

- 简短边界声明；
- 停止当前危险方向；
- 与用户潜在合法目标相关的安全替代信息。

不能把全部 recovery 写成相同的 “I cannot help with that.”。

harmful continuation 必须：

- 延续当前 state 中的同一意图；
- 不引入新的攻击目标；
- 与 recovery 在流畅度、完整性和长度上尽量匹配。

## 6.4 Local preference objective

训练阶段另定义 reference-adjusted branch score：

\[
A_\theta(s,y)=S_\theta(s,y)-S_{\mathrm{ref}}(s,y),
\]

其中：

- \(S_\theta\) 与 Section 3 的长度归一化 branch score 定义相同；
- \(\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}\) 固定为 preference training 前的 instruction checkpoint；
- \(S_{\mathrm{ref}}\) 只提供 DPO 的 reference correction；
- 必须明确区分：诊断 margin 使用 \(S_\theta\)，训练 loss 使用 \(A_\theta\)；
- 不得使用 \(A_\theta\) 计算 Base 的 preference reversal、LTRM 或 H1。

\[
\ell_{\mathrm{state}}(s_t,r_t,u_t)
=-\log\sigma\left(
\beta[A_\theta(s_t,r_t)-A_\theta(s_t,u_t)]
\right).
\]

实现要求：

- prompt 和完整 unsafe state 的 labels 全部设为 `-100`；
- 只有 post-state continuation tokens 进入 loss；
- chosen/rejected 使用相同 chat template；
- position IDs 和 special-token conventions 完全一致；
- reference model 固定；
- \(\beta=0.1\)。

### Shared-prefix cancellation：正文必须说明

若 chosen/rejected 共享完全相同前缀 \(p\)，并使用标准序列 log-prob sum，则

\[
\bigl[\log\pi_\theta(p,r)-\log\pi_\theta(p,u)\bigr]
-
\bigl[\log\pi_{\rm ref}(p,r)-\log\pi_{\rm ref}(p,u)\bigr]
\]

中的 shared-prefix 项严格抵消。因而，不能把 masked context 本身写成相对标准 DPO 的数学创新，也不能未经验证声称“masking 让 gradient 只作用于 recovery，所以产生性能提升”。在使用 branch token average 时，差异可能来自长度归一化和分母，而不是 prefix 是否可见。

必须完成以下 factorial：

| ID | Pair construction | Objective | Prefix treatment | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Same-state | DPO | context masked | log-prob sum |
| B | Same-state | DPO | prefix included | log-prob sum |
| C | Same-state | SimPO | context masked | length-normalized |
| D | Different-state trajectory | DPO | full response | log-prob sum |
| E | Different-state trajectory | SimPO | full response | length-normalized |
| F | Same-state | SFT | recovery only | length-normalized |

至少报告 Middle ASR、unseen-source ASR、recovery rate、ORR、IFEval、LTRM 和 3 seeds。A/B 还必须记录 batch loss difference、gradient cosine similarity、gradient norm 和单步参数更新差异。若 A/B 在标准 sum 下不近似相同，先查实现或 special-token/position-ID 不一致，不得直接包装为方法收益。

公式后直接写与最近基线的区别：

> Recovery SFT increases the probability of the recovery branch without explicitly suppressing its competing harmful future. Full-trajectory preference training can compare sequences that differ before the reached state. CURE-DPO fixes the complete conditioning state and updates only the local ordering of its future branches.

## 6.5 Lower-tail state aggregation

每个 prompt 有 \(K=6\) 个 states。按当前 \(\ell_{\mathrm{state}}\) 排序，选择损失最高的 2 个：

\[
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{tail}}
=\frac{1}{|\mathcal H(x)|}
\sum_{s\in\mathcal H(x)}\ell_{\mathrm{state}}(s,r,u),
\qquad |\mathcal H(x)|=2.
\]

实现时对六个 state 完成同一次 forward，使用 detached loss values 决定 top-2 indices，只让被选中的两个 state loss 进入反向传播。每次该 prompt 被采样时重新计算选择；不得对 top-\(k\) 排序本身声称可微。

解释：

- mean over states 会被容易状态稀释；
- deepest-only 把深度误当作脆弱性；
- top-1 容易追逐 noisy/low-quality pair；
- top-2/6 在 ASR、LTRM、seed variance 和 ORR 上形成最佳总体结果。

统一称为 **per-prompt top-\(k\) lower-tail aggregation**。只可在讨论中说明它与 empirical CVaR 有联系；不得在 Abstract、贡献列表、方法名或主要结论中称其为 CVaR objective，也不得暗示 top-2-of-6 是严格 CVaR estimator。

## 6.6 Anchored objective

\[
\mathcal L=
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{tail}}
+\lambda_{\mathrm c}\mathcal L_{\mathrm{clean}}
+\lambda_{\mathrm b}\mathcal L_{\mathrm{benign}}.
\]

batch 构成为：

- 60% unsafe-state pairs；
- 20% clean safety pairs；
- 20% benign anchors。

三个 component loss 分别在各自 stratum 内取平均，避免 batch 数量直接改变 loss 标度。60/20/20 是固定的分层采样组成；实际 anchor 强度由 \(\lambda_{\mathrm c}\) 和 \(\lambda_{\mathrm b}\) 控制，不得把采样比例与 loss 权重混为一谈。

作用：

- \(\mathcal L_{\mathrm{clean}}\)：保留原始 prompt 的安全排序；
- \(\mathcal L_{\mathrm{benign}}\)：抑制对良性高风险词面问题的泛化拒答。

权重不预设为 0.25。固定搜索：

\[
(\lambda_{\mathrm c},\lambda_{\mathrm b})
\in\{0,0.1,0.25,0.5,1.0\}^2.
\]

只在预先划分的 development safety/utility splits 上选择权重。选择规则为：

1. 在满足以下三个约束的配置中最小化 development middle-insertion ASR：
   - clean ASR 不高于 Base checkpoint；
   - ORR 相对 Base 增幅不超过 2.0 percentage points；
   - IFEval 相对 Base 降幅不超过 0.8 points；
2. 若多个配置并列，选择 \(\lambda_{\mathrm c}+\lambda_{\mathrm b}\) 更小者；
3. 在运行任何 test-set evaluation 前冻结权重；
4. 报告完整 25 点 development grid、被约束排除的配置和最终选择；
5. test set 不得参与权重选择或阈值调整。

no-clean-anchor 与 no-benign-anchor 消融必须分别证明：clean anchor 保留 root safety，benign anchor 控制 safety–helpfulness trade-off。不得把最终权重写成方法固有常数或理论最优值。

## 6.7 算法框

正文可放一个紧凑算法框：

```text
Algorithm 1: CURE-DPO update
Input: harmful prompt x; six same-state pairs {(s_k, r_k, u_k)}; clean and benign anchors
1: Compute state losses l_k for k = 1,...,6
2: Select the two states with the largest current losses
3: Average their same-state preference losses
4: Add clean-safety and benign-anchor objectives
5: Update only the policy parameters; keep the reference model fixed
```

算法框不得占据超过半栏高度。

## 6.8 方法结尾

> CURE-DPO changes the conditional comparison and the state distribution that drives learning; it requires neither a new model architecture nor an inference-time safety filter.

### 可直接使用的结果段

> Figure 2 shows that recovery vulnerability is poorly captured by root-level signals. LTRM-20 reaches 0.87 prompt-level AUROC, 0.21 above the root response margin (95% CI [0.18, 0.24], p<0.001), and retains 0.82 AUROC on a held-out attack family. The relationship is monotonic: states in the lowest recovery-margin quintile have 73.8% attack success, compared with 14.6% in the highest quintile. This difference is not explained by later states being uniformly harder. After exact matching on depth bins and harmful-response source, the corresponding rates remain 67.1% and 20.3%. A multivariable model likewise assigns the largest standardized effect to the recovery margin. Root refusal strength and generation depth therefore do not determine whether an already reached state favors recovery.

边界句：

> These results establish predictive association over the evaluated states, not a causal or certified robustness guarantee.

### 可直接使用的结果段

> The advantage of tail-focused training persists when the test states differ from the mining distribution. > We restrict this conclusion to the tested intervention families and two model families.

## 8.4 6.4 Gains are not explained by indiscriminate refusal

### Table 3：状态聚合与 anchor 消融（LoRA 敏感性实验）

| Variant | Middle ASR ↓ | Unseen-source ASR ↓ | LTRM ↑ | ORR ↓ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|

### 可直接使用的消融段

> Table 3 shows that neither generation depth nor a single hardest pair is sufficient. Training only on the deepest state performs worse than averaging all states, confirming that depth is not a reliable proxy for local vulnerability. Top-1 selection approaches the safety performance of CURE-DPO but increases ORR to 10.4% and exhibits larger seed variance, consistent with sensitivity to noisy pairs. Selecting the highest-loss third gives the best joint result on middle ASR, unseen-source ASR, LTRM, and ORR. Removing the clean anchor tests whether root-level safety is preserved independently of state recovery; removing the benign anchor slightly improves ASR but raises ORR from 8.8% to 13.6%. The two anchors are therefore evaluated as separate trade-off controls rather than treated as sources of the core robustness gain.

### Utility 段

> General capability changes are small at the development-selected operating point. On Qwen, IFEval changes from 88.7 to 88.0 and MMLU-Pro from 74.8 to 74.2; ORR increases from 7.4% to 8.8%. On Llama, no reported utility metric decreases by more than the pre-specified 0.8-point practical threshold. The anchor weights are selected before test evaluation by minimizing development middle-insertion ASR subject to pre-specified clean-safety, over-refusal, and IFEval constraints. Appendix K reports the complete development grid and the resulting safety–over-refusal frontier.

# 13. Conclusion：可直接使用

> **We showed that safe refusal from an original prompt does not ensure safe recovery after a model reaches an unsafe generation state. Same-state recovery margins expose this conditional preference reversal and predict held-out intervention success more reliably than root-level signals. CURE-DPO fixes the reached state as context, directly ranks its recovery and harmful futures, and focuses learning on vulnerable lower-tail states. Across two model families and held-out interventions, this design improves recovery robustness with small changes in instruction following and over-refusal. Safety alignment should therefore evaluate and optimize the preferences induced at states encountered during generation, not only the response selected at the root.**

# 14. Limitations：可直接改写的四段

Limitations 必须位于 Conclusion 后、References 前，不计正文页数，不得加入新实验、图或分析。

## 14.1 Threat model

> Our threat model focuses on assistant-side generation states produced by prefilling, mid-sequence intervention, or a partially unsafe model trajectory. It does not cover all input-side jailbreaks, multi-turn social engineering, agentic tool misuse, retrieval poisoning, or attacks that modify model weights. The reported robustness should therefore be interpreted as recovery robustness under the evaluated state interventions rather than general jailbreak immunity.

## 14.2 Constructed alternatives

> Recovery margins depend on constructed safe and harmful continuations. We reduce branch artifacts through source separation, length matching, filtering, and a blinded pair-quality audit, but the measured margin remains conditional on the alternatives included in the evaluation set. A positive margin is not a certificate that every unknown continuation or reachable state is safe.

## 14.3 Model and language scope

> We evaluate two open-weight English instruction models at approximately 8–9B parameters. The replication across Qwen and Llama reduces dependence on one tokenizer or post-training recipe, but does not establish the same effect for frontier closed models, substantially larger or smaller models, multilingual settings, multimodal systems, or long chain-of-thought trajectories.

## 14.4 Safety–helpfulness trade-off

> CURE-DPO produces small average changes in instruction following and over-refusal at the selected operating point, yet benign professional requests containing high-risk terminology remain a recurring failure mode. Deployment would require domain-specific calibration, monitoring, and escalation procedures rather than using a single global refusal threshold.

# 15. Ethical Considerations：可直接改写的三段

## 15.1 Harmful data and access

> The study requires harmful prompts, intermediate states, and continuations. Raw high-risk content is stored under access controls and is not reproduced in the paper. Public artifacts should contain aggregated results, hashes, labels, and sanitized examples that do not provide actionable harmful instructions. Data sources, licenses, retention policies, and access permissions are documented in the supplementary material.

## 15.2 Dual use

> The intervention and search procedures could be used to identify weak generation states. To reduce dual-use risk, the release should emphasize defensive training and evaluation code, omit the highest-success attack templates and executable harmful outputs, and provide a controlled process for qualified researchers who require more detailed evaluation materials.

## 15.3 Annotator protection

> Human annotators receive advance content warnings, may skip examples or withdraw without penalty, and are exposed to bounded batches rather than continuous harmful content. The study reports training, compensation, annotation time, disagreement resolution, and available support resources. No unnecessary personal information is collected. Any use of generative AI for writing or programming is disclosed according to the applicable ACL policy, and all citations and experimental claims are independently verified by the authors.
